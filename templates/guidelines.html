TERMS & COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Powerful tools. Responsible use.

These guidelines explain the expected use of Gargand and its website.

Server owners

Give Gargand only the Discord permissions needed for the features you enable. Review role hierarchy and channel access after setup.

Moderation

Use bans, kicks, timeouts and warnings consistently with your community rules. Review automated actions and keep staff accountable.

Security features

Anti-raid and anti-nuke protections can affect members and server configuration. Test settings carefully and keep trusted staff roles up to date.

Privacy

Do not request passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information through tickets, verification or feedback forms.

Website accounts

Keep your Discord account secure. The dashboard should only be used by people who have permission to manage the selected server.

Availability

Features may change as Gargand is updated. Website announcements are the place to check for release notes and known issues.
