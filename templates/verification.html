GARGAND • VERIFICATION

Quiet on the web. Stronger on Discord.

Website visitors can browse normally. Gargand adds extra checks only where a form or a new Discord member needs protection.

◉

Website: no puzzle

Normal form submissions receive a signed browser-session check and same-site request validation. Short request limits and hidden spam traps help reduce automated abuse. There is no CAPTCHA and visitors do not need to click a verification button.

🔐

Discord: risk-aware challenge

When server verification is configured, new members receive an Unverified role and complete a private, changing phrase challenge before Gargand grants Verified access. The challenge gets longer when account age or a join surge raises the risk score.

⏱

Abuse limits, clear review

Challenges expire after five minutes. Three incorrect answers trigger a 15-minute cooldown and repeated starts are slowed down. Risk signals are recorded for moderators; they change the challenge strength, not whether a member is automatically banned.

SERVER SETUP

Configure a real access gate

Run !setup, choose the verification channel, then confirm the bot’s role is above Unverified and Verified. Set Unverified to read-only in your regular channels; Gargand does not rewrite every existing room’s permissions.
