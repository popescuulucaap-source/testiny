Staged raid response

With Anti-raid enabled, Gargand records a watch alert at five joins in 30 seconds. At 12 joins, an optional setting can start a 10-minute posting lockdown. It saves the current @everyone posting overrides and restores them automatically; admins can also use !raidlockdown 10m and !raidrelease.

✦

Auto-Mod

Configure spam, flood, mention and filtered-word checks, choose a response, and send moderation events to your log channel.

⌘

Searchable case history

Successful moderation actions get a case ID with the moderator, member, reason, channel and expiry where applicable. Staff can look up !case 123, list !cases, and search the website’s case and incident center when shared database storage is configured.
