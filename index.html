Testiny

A Discord moderation bot. Ban or kick with one command, message the member automatically, and give banned members a way to appeal.

https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=1546576985168285786&permissions=8&integration_type=0&scope=bot Add to Discord See commands

Commands

  • /ban @member reasonor !ban

    Bans the member. Before the ban, Testiny sends them a message with the server name and an Appeal button that takes them to your appeal server.

    Staff only
  • /kick @member reasonor !kick

    Kicks the member and sends them a message saying they were kicked from your server.

    Staff only
  • /setupor !setup

    Opens a menu where you pick your staff role, your logs channel and your appeal server.

    Needs Manage Server

Get started

  1. Add Testiny to your server.Use the Add to Discord button and allow Ban Members and Kick Members.
  2. Add it to your appeal server too.Testiny needs the Create Invite permission there.
  3. Run /setup.Choose the staff role, the logs channel and the appeal server, then press Save.
Testiny Every ban and kick is posted to your logs channel.
